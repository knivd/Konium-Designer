Routes are shaped after routing, not while they are searched for (src/model/reflow.ts): each chain of free tracks between pads, vias and branches is pulled tight twice (the second time against its neighbours as they then lie) and its bends then made arcs, every other route standing as an obstacle in the shape it has at that moment; a route keeps its ends, layer, width and clearances and never gets longer. Where: **Preferences › PCB Editor › Interactive Routing › Routing Style** (Corners or Free-Flowing), followed by Route › Auto Route (not its orthogonal strategy), by the Smart Router for the classes whose plan says nothing, and by a route placed by hand (shaped as it is placed); a class's style in the Smart Router's plan (`flowing` or `corners`); **Route › Make Free-Flowing** for routes that are there, the selected ones or all, in one undo step.
